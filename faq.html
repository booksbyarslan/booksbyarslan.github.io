Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with Books by Arslan.

Can you clean up my existing books?

Yes. We can review your existing books, identify and correct bookkeeping issues, reconcile accounts, and bring your records up to date.

Do you offer monthly bookkeeping?

Yes. Monthly bookkeeping is one of our primary services. We can handle your books on an ongoing basis so your records stay organized and up to date.

Can you work with my CPA or tax preparer?

Absolutely. We can maintain organized, accurate books and provide the information your CPA or tax professional needs for tax preparation and reporting.

Do I need to provide everything for you to get started?

We’ll let you know exactly what we need during onboarding. Typically, this includes access to your bookkeeping software, bank and credit card accounts, and any relevant business records.

How often will I hear from you?

We work directly with our clients and keep communication simple. We can establish a communication routine that works for your business and provide updates when needed.

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your business, transaction volume, number of accounts, and the condition of your existing books. Contact us for a free consultation and we'll discuss the scope before providing a quote.

Can you take over from my current bookkeeper?

Yes. We can review your existing setup and take over your bookkeeping with a structured transition.

How do I get started?

Simply contact us or book a free consultation. We'll learn about your business, understand your bookkeeping needs, and discuss how we can help.

Where is Books by Arslan based?

Books by Arslan is a remote bookkeeping business based in Pakistan, serving clients in the United States and internationally. We work across time zones and communicate directly with our clients online.

Why should I choose Books by Arslan?

Books by Arslan combines hands-on bookkeeping experience with a strong accounting background. Our focus is simple: keeping your books organized, accurate, and useful so you can focus on running your business.

Do you have accounting knowledge?

Yes. Books by Arslan combines hands-on bookkeeping experience with a strong accounting background to help keep your records organized, accurate, and useful.

Can you handle accrual-basis accounting?

Yes. We can work with accrual-basis bookkeeping and help record transactions in the appropriate accounting period, maintain organized records, and prepare useful financial reports.
